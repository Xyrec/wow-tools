1.

Elemental Shaman Pre-Raid Best in Slot List

This is the pre-raid gear we recommend for Elemental Shamans, setting you up to tackle the initial raids of TBC, such as Karazhan. The gear choices in the tables below follow our stat guide priority.

If your character is ready to jump into raiding content, then see our current best in slot list below.
