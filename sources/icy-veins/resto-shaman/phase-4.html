TBC Tier 6 Elemental Shaman Tier Set

Restoration Shaman Tier 6, the Skyshatter Raiment, is obtained from the following bosses:

  • Helm — Archimonde in Hyjal Summit
  • Chest — Illidan Stormrage in Black Temple
  • Legs — The Illidari Council in Black Temple
  • Shoulders — Mother Shahraz in Black Temple
  • Gloves — Azgalor in Hyjal Summit

Its set bonuses are:

  • 2 pieces — lowers the cost of Chain Heal by 10%. A very powerful effect due to the high amount of Chain Heals cast, this bonus allows you to be more reckless with your casts before you run out of Mana.
  • 4 pieces — increases the healing of Chain Heal by 5%. This is a multiplicative bonus to Chain Heal and a large healing increase. When combined with the 2-set and a good amount of Spell Haste, you will be able to increase your throughput massively when compared to Tier 5 levels.

Phase 4 Best in Slot Table

Leatherworking is still a great profession, especially if you are your group's dedicated drummer, and Windhawk gear can fill in for any slots you were unlucky with.

Enchanting is also great to unlock Spell Healing enchants on rings, and Jewelcrafting is a viable alternative for improved gems.

Finally, Engineering is also a fine choice, enabling explosives and a strong helmet in Living Replicator Specs.

In order to make the best use possible out of long cooldown trinkets, such as Scarab Brooch, swap them into your gear set when they are off-cooldown and replace them with others during their cooldown.
