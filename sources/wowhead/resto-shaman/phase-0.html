In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Restoration Shaman Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Restoration Shaman Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Shaman Healing in Pre-Raid

Phase 1 gear sets are highly dependent on whether you choose Leatherworking as a Profession or not. The Windhawk set is extremely good, but requires Leatherworking. Not only is the Windhawk set pre-raid BiS, but it can also be considered Phase 1 BiS as well.

Many items can be considered BiS for each slot, depending on whether you play an aggressive Healing Power style or a Mana considerate style that relies more on stats like Spirit and MP5. The sections below offer detailed information for each slot.

Should Restoration Shaman Healer Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?

Restoration Shamans favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant, Greater Inscription of Faith, from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative, Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Shamans tend to have Leatherworking, there are various Leatherworking recipes from both factions, but nothing noteworthy enough to influence your decision.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Weapons for Restoration Shaman in TBC Classic Pre-Raid

It is generally better to use a 1-handed weapon paired with an offhand instead of a 2-handed weapon.

While Gladiator's Salvation is the best pre-raid option, it is hard to obtain via PvP. Hand of Eternity is a great BoE pre-raid option available through buying or crafting via Blacksmithing. However, there are better options in raid, and the gold investment might not be worth it. Shockwave Truncheon is a cheaper alternative and still very solid. Keep in mind that Heroic dungeons might be very challenging early on.
