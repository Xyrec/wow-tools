    Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Shaman Healing in Phase 5

    The Tier 6 (Skyshatter Raiment) set bonuses should be obtained as quickly as possible, as they synergize together and are extremely strong. Due to the new Tier 6 pieces introduced in Sunwell Plateau having very strong stats, you will generally want to prioritize using these pieces for your tier bonuses, while using off-pieces on the head, legs, gloves, and chest.

    Spell haste is a particularly strong stat that increases your throughput and ability to reactively heal. This does come at a cost to your mana pool if you are spamming heals without pause, however.

    Should Restoration Shaman Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?

    Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    • Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for healing.

    • Scryers offer a decent staff at Revered, Seer's Cane. This is okay for starters, but almost any loot you can get at 70 is better, including cheap auction house BoEs such as The Essence Focuser + Crystal Pulse Shield.

    • Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

    If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

    Best in Slot Trinkets for Restoration Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 5

    In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

    Redeemer's Alchemist Stone is the best trinket available in Phase 5, as long as you are using mana pots on cooldown, but it requires Alchemy to craft and equip. Thus, Eye of Gruul, Memento of Tyrande, and Glimmering Naaru Sliver are the best trinkets for a mana conservation profile, while Essence of the Martyr and Direbrew Hops are still the best trinkets if you aren't having any mana issues, sporting strong passive stats and a powerful on-use effect that allows you to really pump out the healing when it is active!

    Best in Slot Weapons for Restoration Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 5

    Staves and other two-handed weapons generally grant less total healing than an MH+OH combo, and also lack the extra armor and block a shield provides. Thus, we would strongly recommend you avoid two-handed weapons unless the amount of stats on the weapon is way better than what you are currently using.

    Hammer of Sanctification is the ultimate Phase 5 weapon, having the highest stats in every category by a landslide.

    Alternatively, Archon's Gavel is also very strong, as is Crystal Spire of Karabor, whose proc has unfortunately yet to prove itself on the logs. Dark Blessing is also extremely strong, and PvP Shamans can get the new PvP weapon, Brutal Gladiator's Salvation.

    Best in Slot Shields & Offhands for Restoration Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 5

    Whenever possible, try to use shields if they have comparable stats to the offhands at your disposal, as they can be enchanted with 12 Intellect and you can use Greater Ward of Shielding to increase their healing contribution beyond what any comparable offhand item could provide. They will also make any physical damage that comes your way much more manageable.

    Unfortunately, the best offhand in this phase is not a shield but Book of Highborne Hymns, which has superior healing stats to any shield in the game. Do take into account that if you are using a Greater Ward of Shielding on every pull, the shield alternatives Aegis of Angelic Fortune, Bastion of Light, and Felstone Bulwark are still better.
