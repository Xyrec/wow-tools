This guide will list the recommended gear for Protection Paladin Tank to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

      Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 4

      In contrast to phase 3, phase 4 is not a very major phase for Protection Paladins who are already established and raiding the current content; it is an incredible phase for those who want to pick up Protection Paladin and don't want to get into the potential hassle of raiding 25-man. Zul'Aman is, by design, a very fast and efficient raid, and this phase offers very good, but not quite as good as tier 6 gear.

      One of the prevailing themes around phase 4 gear for Protection Paladins is that they are very similar to existing phase 3 items in their respective slots, but usually end up just slightly short. This means you may treat a lot of the items from Zul'aman like Black Temple & Mount Hyjal non-tier sidegrades, and given how powerful our tier pieces are from phase 3, it means not much is going to change on the BiS end of things, but mostly that the road to BiS may look drastically different.

      This doesn't mean that Phase 4 is not an exciting time for already geared Protection Paladins. Phase 4 gear, in contrast to Phase 3, allots its stats very differently, focusing on very efficient block rating over the more prevalent and valuable dodge that's on tier 6 and Phase 3 gear. This means that phase 4 may help Protection Paladins bridge gaps between existing sets in avoidance and is also very valuable for those that focus on farming content, since the combination of Block Value and Block Rating goes hand in hand with Hobbs way of pulling.

      As usual, all gear is valuable, and we strongly recommend that you do not throw out new acquisitions without mindfulness. Likewise, avoidance, especially since it is frankly unintuitive and the game does a poor job of conveying what you need to do, it very easy to make mistakes. We recommend the Weakaura Tankadin III to assist you in surveying your equipped stats.

        Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 4

        For Protection Paladins, the stat differences between the blue shoulder enchants for each faction are pretty marginal. We recommend that you go with Aldor in Phase 4, due to having an overall better quality of stats than Scryer for Protection Paladin needs.

        Comparing the defensive stats between both shoulder enchants, Greater Inscription of Warding is the superior option by a small margin due to the prevalence of dodge. It is worth noting that Greater Inscription of the Knight offers more overall avoidance in the form of Block Rating, but this is largely wasted in phase 4.

        Aldor spell power enchant Greater Inscription of Discipline also offers better threat stats over the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Orb, giving a clear overall advantage to Aldor. However, those who went Scryer early on will not see any major disadvantages for doing so and staying with the Blood Elf faction.

        Best in Slot Librams for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 4

        Libram of Repentance is the recommended Phase 4 avoidance BiS libram due to providing +5.33% Block Chance while Holy Shield is active, making it easier for tanks to attain uncrushability, which is necessary for tanking raid bosses.
