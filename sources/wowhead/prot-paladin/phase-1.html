    Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 1

    Best in Slot gear for tanks should also include an asterisk that you'll also look to get as much bag space as possible, as your "best" gear may change encounter to encounter. For tanks, there are scenarios where you would want to put on more gear for Threat, and there are encounters where you'll want to gear more towards Mitigation / Survivability. Because of that, we must note that there is not "one" bis list for tanks.

    When working on your tanking build, the minimum goals should be the following:

    • Immunity to Critical Hits - Crit Cap/ Crit Immunity (490 Defense)

    • Immunity to Crushing Blows - Crush Cap / Avoidance Cap (+102.4% Miss/Dodge/Parry/Block with Holy Shield)

    • 10k+ HP with full enchants/gems, unbuffed

    You'll notice a very healthy amount of PvP gear on here, both from Honor Points and Arena Points. We discuss this in our Stat Priority guide, but it should be remembered how Resilience works when looking at our aforementioned goals above. Resilience, like Defense, contributes towards Crit Immunity. Defense, however, also provides Avoidance through additional Dodge/Parry/Block/Miss, whereas Resilience does not. A high amount of PvP gear would work in Heroic dungeons and trash mobs in Raids, since the gear provides high Stamina and high Spell Power, and these mobs cannot crush. It is not advisable to main tank bosses in raids in full Resilience, as you will not be Avoidance Cap doing so; however, you can fit in some PvP gear into your gearset and still be Crush cap, while having better threat from the PvP gear's high Spell Power.

    Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 1

    For Protection Paladins, the main differences between the factions come in the early game about quest rewards and rep vendor items that can help you gear up much faster for tanking heroic dungeons and Phase 1 raids. In terms of rep gain, the Scryers tend to be an easier faction to gain reputation with, so in that respect, Scryers are the superior option for those who want to get their shoulder enchants as fast as possible.

    For the shoulder enchants, Greater Inscription of the Knight from the Scryers will edge out Greater Inscription of Warding, although marginal, it still offers more towards Crit reduction and overall Avoidance. The Aldor do offer a really solid tank chest with Vindicator's Hauberk, but this can easily be replaced with other gear. And on that note, the Scryers offer two relatively strong Threat pieces with Scryer's Bloodgem and Seer's Signet, and offer a really strong tank necklace with Thalodien's Charm, which you could be wearing longer than the tank chest option offered from The Aldor.

    If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Protection Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

    Best in Slot Trinkets for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 1

    The various trinkets listed below fulfill a wide variety of roles, such as survivability, threat generation, and general utility. Similarly to Rings, this is another slot where we will find ourselves consistently swapping out certain trinkets for certain encounters. And also similar to the Ring option, in a basic setting, we'd recommend one trinket for Threat and the other for Survival / Mitigation.

    For a "best" pairing, we would recommend Figurine of the Colossus and Icon of the Silver Crescent.

    Crit/Crush Reduction + Avoidance/Mitigation Trinkets

    As mentioned above, the recommended BiS trinket for Surivability is Figurine of the Colossus due to its high amount of Shield Block Rating and strong on-use effect.
    Moroes' Lucky Pocket Watch is also a very powerful trinket, and does provide more Avoidance should we need it, but as a block-based tank, we will look to the latter vs the former.

    Several other options in the drop-down menu are also powerful in their own right, as should you need more Defense in your early gearing to achieve Crit Immunity (490 Defense), then you would need to equip one of those over the two former mentioned, as unfortunately, neither provides any Defense.

    Stamina Trinkets

    Stamina trinkets are primarily recommended for maximizing HP only after Defense Cap (490 Defense) and Uncrushability (102.4% Avoidance + Block) have been reached. Gobling Rocket Launcher is the recommended BiS trinket for stamina, due to its high stamina and threat generation potential provided by its on-use.

    Threat Generation Trinkets

    Trinkets that provide Spell Power bonuses are recommended for tanks that prioritize threat generation over survivability.

    Icon of the Silver Crescent is the recommended BiS trinket for threat generation for multiple reasons: It's uncontested, gives a passive strong amount of Spell Power, and has a powerful On-Use for great snap-threat at the start of encounters or during threat-wipe mechanics.

    Eye of Magtheridon is technically more powerful; however, it is a highly contested item and relies on procs, whereas the former is a controlled on-use from the player. Should you be able to obtain this, take it.

    There is a large variety of other trinkets in all three categories for Tank Paladins, should you not have access to the recommendations above.
