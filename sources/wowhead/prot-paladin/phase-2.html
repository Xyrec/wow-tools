Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Protection Paladin Tank in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Protection Paladin Tank to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.

Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 2

Going into Phase 2, we'll be looking to replace a healthy amount of our Phase 1 BiS gear. We don't actively seek Tier 5 specifically for any particular set bonus, although the 4-set bonus is powerful in its own right; we will look to upgrade some of our Tier 4 for Tier 5, as the raw stat upgrades are too powerful to ignore. With that being said, we are still looking to keep our 2-set bonus from our Tier 4, as the damage increase provided to our seals is a very strong bonus that we don't want to discard just yet.

There are an extremely powerful couple of pieces that are unfortunately locked behind Blacksmithing. Since we typically have not recommended this profession as one of the top two to pick for Protection Paladin, we have also included the next best options.

As we learned from Phase 1, although we do not want to ignore threat generation, should we find ourselves in a main tank situation for raid bosses, we always want to prioritize Survivability first. However, it should be noted that one specific encounter, being Morogrim Tidewalker in Serpentshrine Cavern, is where the Protection Paladin shines the brightest as the designated off-tank. Since we won't find ourselves tanking the boss in this encounter, typically, and focusing on the add waves, we should gear heavily more towards Spell Power and threat generation, and discard the stat goal of Avoidance Cap for this encounter. We won't mention a specific "set" to build for that encounter, but consider your gearing options and make sure you look towards having a heavy threat generation set when you make your way to that boss.

With that being said, as we mentioned in the Phase 1 BIS guide, there is no true BiS for tanking. There are various builds that are better suited for particular scenarios.

For Phase 2, many of the best pieces of gear for Protection Paladin tanks will come from Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, and Arenas.

Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 2

For Protection Paladins, the stat differences between the blue shoulder enchants for each faction are pretty marginal, with Scryers Greater Inscription of the Knight barely winning out vs Aldors Greater Inscription of Warding. The main differences between the factions come in the early game with regard to quest rewards and rep vendor items that can help you gear up much faster for tanking heroic dungeons and Phase 2 raids. In terms of reputation gain, The Scryers tend to be an easier faction to gain reputation with, so in that respect, Scryers are the superior option for those who want to obtain their shoulder enchants as quickly as possible.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Protection Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Head Armor for Protection Paladin Tank in TBC Classic Phase 2

Crystalforge Faceguard is the recommended Phase 2 BiS head piece due to providing high stamina, spellpower, and defensive stats. The helm also provides +2.41% Shield Block Chance, which provides additional flexibility in how other slots are used.

Some other great options include Faceguard of Determination for its incredibly high avoidance stats, or the Engineer exclusive Tankatronic Goggles for its ridiculously high amount of Stamina offered when paired with gems, as it can provide a total of 97 Stamina!

Gem Sidenote for Helms:
For helms with a Stamina gem bonus, it is worth considering slotting in epic hybrid gems such as Enduring Chrysoprase, Glowing Tanzanite, or Regal Tanzanite instead of a standard solid blue gem in order to get a strong combination of Stamina as well as other secondary stats.

Best in Slot Hand Armor for Protection Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 2

Royal Gauntlets of Silvermoon are the Phase 2 BiS gauntlets for survivability due to the large amount of Stamina they can provide, along with the hefty amount of Dodge they include. This high amount of survivability, however, comes at the cost of providing no benefit for threat generation, unfortunately.

Crystalforge Handguards are the Phase 2 BiS gauntlets for threat generation due to providing a large amount of Spell Power alongside a fair amount of survivability. These gloves come nowhere close to providing as much Stamina compared to the former. However, the high Block Rating does provide more room for a Paladin Tank to maintain uncrushability while optimizing for threat generation in other slots, and it will come from a much earlier boss in progression, whereas the former comes from typically the last boss you'll defeat from the two raids. Should your threat generation be comfortable and your raid progression be at a healthy pace, you should forgo these for the Survivability option.

Best in Slot Waist Armor for Protection Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 2

Girdle of the Invulnerable is the recommended Phase 2 BiS belt for those without Blacksmithing. Although this belt provides no Spell Power for threat generation and has slightly less Stamina compared to Belt of the Guardian, this belt provides an extremely high amount of avoidance that can significantly increase the Effective HP of tanks.

Belt of the Guardian is the Phase 2 BiS belt for threat generation. This belt provides a fair amount of Spell Power as well as the most Stamina for a plate waist piece with gems. The main downside of this item is that it does not provide any additional avoidance or Block Rating except for its Defense, so the loss in avoidance/block is going to have to be compensated for in other slots. Girdle of Valorous Deeds continues to be a powerful and viable alternative to this belt, especially because of its high block chance, although it does not have any gem slots.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 2

The various trinkets listed below fulfill a wide variety of roles, such as survivability, threat generation, and general utility.

Crit/Crush Reduction + Avoidance/Mitigation Trinkets

The recommended BiS trinket for survivability is Figurine of the Colossus along with Moroes' Lucky Pocket Watch due to the large amount of avoidance they both provide. The passive effect for the latter provides approximately +2.00% Dodge, and the active effect provides about +15.9% Dodge. Brooch of the Immortal King, Adamantine Figurine, and Dabiri's Enigma are still recommended for maintaining Defense cap (490 Defense) for raids and heroic dungeons.

Stamina Trinkets

The Goblin Engineering trinkets will still be the best Stamina trinkets in the game. It is recommended to start your raid progression with this trinket on.

Threat Generation Trinkets

Eye of Magtheridon and Icon of the Silver Crescent will continue to be your best Threat trinket options; the latter is much easier to obtain and has a powerful on-use activation for strong snap threat on pulls and threat wipe mechanics.

Libram of Repentance is the recommended Phase 2 BiS libram due to providing +5.33% Block Chance while Holy Shield is up, making it easier for tanks to attain uncrushability, which is necessary for tanking heroic dungeons and raids.
