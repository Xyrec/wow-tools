Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Protection Paladin Tank in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Protection Paladin Tank to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

      Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 3

      Phase 3 is a period of major upgrades for Protection Paladin, with some of the best itemization the game has to offer. Lightbringer Armor offers substantial upgrades in every slot, with a significant 4set that will see our already strong AoE capabilities reach new heights, phase 3 also introduces to us very strong off set items such as Faceplate of the Impenetrable and Praetorian's Legguards which can rival their tier counterparts in many respects.

      These sets are specifically modeled on Archimonde due to his unique place as a phase 3 endboss, he is the hardest and one of the fastest hitters in the tier outside of Illidan. He is an imperfect example, but a very interesting one to build your gear around, because if you can tank Archimonde, you can tank every other boss in phase 3 with the same gear, save Illidan himself who follows a different and unique ruleset.

      Illidan Stormrage will often cast Shear, this ability can not miss, but it can be dodged, parried and blocked. The reason this is important is because it will reduce your max HP by 60% for the duration of the debuff it applies, as such you need to have a cumulative 101.8% avoidance whenever he casts Shear so that you will never be hit by it.

      Because protection paladins are reliant on layers of avoidance to reach requisite status to tank bosses, it is highly recommended that you do not immediately throw away gear as you get new pieces. Though an item may be listed on this guide as an upgrade, it may not immediately fit well into your current set. You will often have to make small changes to your sets to accommodate “upgrades” to maintain proper amounts of avoidance and crit immunity. To make this easier, we recommend an addon or weakaura that helps you track your avoidance, like Tankadin III.

        Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 3

        For Protection Paladins, the stat differences between the blue shoulder enchants for each faction are pretty marginal, with Scryers Greater Inscription of the Knight barely winning out vs Aldors Greater Inscription of Warding. The main differences between the factions come in the early game with regard to quest rewards and rep vendor items that can help you gear up much faster for tanking heroic dungeons and Phase 2 raids. In terms of reputation gain, The Scryers tend to be an easier faction to gain reputation with, so in that respect, Scryers are the superior option for those who want to obtain their shoulder enchants as quickly as possible.

        If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Protection Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

        At this point in the game, the Aldor spell power enchant Greater Inscription of Discipline also offers better threat stats over the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Orb, giving a clear overall advantage to Aldor. Although this is not a game-breaking choice for those who went Scryer early on for their Phase 1 items.

        Best in Slot Head Armor for Protection Paladin Tank in TBC Classic Phase 3

        We recommend Faceplate of the Impenetrable as your go to off set piece in your head. This provides a major defensive boost for a relatively low cost out of any other headpiece in Phase 3. However, there is a caveat in gearing. Should you acquire Praetorian's Legguards prior to obtaining this, we recommend that you take Lightbringer Faceguard, it's a good helmet which fills most of our needs, but the lack of avoidance is fairly restrictive later down the line, should you want to swap pieces.

        Best in Slot Shoulder Armor for Protection Paladin Tank in TBC Classic Phase 3

        There are no other real options here; Lightbringer Shoulderguards is strictly the best item in the slot. That being said, above certain thresholds, hard avoidance can carry a lot of weight in overall damage reduction, this is why we still give it mentions but still recommend Fortitude of the Scourge as your main shoulder enchant from Sapphiron.

        There are drawbacks to our recommended BiS mitigation ring. Since we have such long cooldowns, finding good sources of hit will see a noticeable impact on your threat output.         Best in Slot Librams for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 3

        Libram of Repentance is the recommended Phase 3 BiS libram due to providing +5.33% Block Chance while Holy Shield is active, making it easier for tanks to attain uncrushability, which is necessary for tanking raid bosses. This libram is especially useful for maintaining immunity against Shear if main tanking Illidan Stormrage.

        Phase 3 introduces a new tanking libram, Tome of the Lightbringer. This libram excels at mitigating damage when tanking large packs of mobs, for example, when tanking trash waves in Hyjal Summit or mobs in Heroic dungeons. With regards to uptime, assuming the paladin tank is casting Judgement every 10 seconds, this libram's proc will be active approximately 50% of the time.

        Libram of the Eternal Rest will add 4.477 damage to your consecration ticks, it's a very low return on the cost so we don't recommend this.
