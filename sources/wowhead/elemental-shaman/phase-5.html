Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Elemental Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Elemental Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

    Best In Slot Gear for Elemental Shaman DPS in Phase 5

    Elemental Shaman's Tier 6 bonuses are strong, and getting four pieces of the set should be one of your main priorities, with three of them being the new tier pieces in Sunwell Plateau. Ideally, you will want your remaining piece to be the shoulders, as it is the slot where you have the least to gain by switching to a Sunwell Plateau gear piece.

    The Skull of Gul'dan is a best in slot trinket for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

    Should Elemental Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?

    Shamans can pick either of the Aldor or the Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    • Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.

    • Aldor also offers a decent staff at Revered, Auchenai Staff. This is okay for starters, but Main Hand / Offhand combinations are better at level 70.

    • Scryers offers one of the best rings, Seer's Signet, at Exalted, and a powerful trinket, Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered, although you probably will not need its high spell hit due to getting so much of it from talents.

    • Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

    If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Elemental Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

    Best in Slot Trinkets for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 5

    In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

    The Skull of Gul'dan is one of the best in slot trinkets for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

    Thus, Shifting Naaru Sliver, The Lightning Capacitor, and Hex Shrunken Head are generally your best trinkets, with the Sliver sporting both high stats and an extremely powerful on use effect, Capacitor benefitting from your frequent spell crits and overload crits, and Shrunken Head having high spell damage, which you can further power up as a DPS cooldown.

    It should also be noted that Classic-era trinkets, such as Mark of the Champion and Neltharion's Tear, are still extremely powerful in TBC and can be used for a long time, especially Mark of the Champion, which stays your best trinket when fighting Demons and Undead.

    BiS The Skull of Gul'dan Drop: Illidan Stormrage (Black Temple)
    BiS The Lightning Capacitor Drop: Terestian Illhoof (Karazhan)
    BiS Hex Shrunken Head Drop: Hex Lord Malacrass (Zul'Aman)
    Special Mark of the Champion Drop: Kel'Thuzad (Classic - Naxxramas)
