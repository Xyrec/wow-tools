This guide will list the recommended gear for Elemental Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

    Best In Slot Gear for Elemental Shaman DPS in Phase 3

    In Phase 3, Elemental Shamans finally get strong tier set bonuses, and getting four pieces of the set should be one of your main priorities. Ideally, you will want your off-set piece to be the legs, as Leggings of Channeled Elements are the strongest off-piece available, but crafting Mantle of Nimble Thought is also a good option, especially if competition for the legs in your guild is high.

    The Skull of Gul'dan is the best in slot trinket for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

    Should Elemental Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?

    Shamans can pick either of the Aldor or the Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    • Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.

    • Aldor also offers a decent staff at Revered, Auchenai Staff. This is okay for starters, but Main Hand / Offhand combinations are better at level 70.

    • Scryers offers one of the best rings, Seer's Signet, at Exalted, and a powerful trinket, Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered, although you probably will not need its high spell hit due to getting so much of it from talents.

    • Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

    If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Elemental Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

    Best in Slot Trinkets for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 3

    In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

    The Skull of Gul'dan is the best in slot trinket for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

    Thus, The Lightning Capacitor and Icon of the Silver Crescent (or Dark Iron Smoking Pipe, the Brewfest caster trinket) are your best trinkets, with The Lightning Capacitor benefitting from your frequent spell crits and overload crits and Icon of the Silver Crescent having high spell damage, which you can further power up as a DPS cooldown. Sextant of Unstable Currents is a fine alternative, but its damage contribution can be quite random.

    It should also be noted that Classic-era trinkets, such as Mark of the Champion and Neltharion's Tear, are still extremely powerful in TBC and can be used for a long time, especially Mark of the Champion, which stays your best trinket when fighting Demons and Undead.

    Best in Slot Weapons for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 3

    Staves and other two-handed weapons generally grant less total spell damage than an MH+OH combo, and also lack the extra armor and block a shield provides, making an MH+OH generally superior if its stats are comparable to the best two-handed weapon available.

    Zhar'doom, Greatstaff of the Devourer is the ultimate Phase 3 weapon, sporting a huge amount of all throughput stats except spell hit, which makes it perfect for Elemental Shamans. Because it is a highly sought-after item, you might want to consider PvPing for the Season 3 Vengeful Gladiator's Gavel or hoping for The Maelstrom's Fury or, to a lesser extent, Hammer of Judgement.
